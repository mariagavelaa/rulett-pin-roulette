Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This document describes the intended privacy practices of Rulett Pin Roulette, a personal project designed to help its owner rediscover saved Pinterest Pins.

1. Responsible party

Project: Rulett Pin Roulette
Responsible person: María Gavela Sanz
Contact: mariagavelasanzsanz@gmail.com

2. Purpose of the application

Rulett is designed for personal use. Its intended purpose is to display a randomly selected Pin from a Pinterest board authorized by its account owner.

3. Pinterest authorization

The planned integration will use Pinterest's official authorization system (OAuth). The application will request only the permissions necessary to read the account owner's boards and Pins.

Rulett will not request or collect Pinterest passwords or session cookies.

4. Data handling

The intended design is to retrieve Pinterest information only when needed to provide the roulette feature. Pinterest information will not be sold, shared with advertising networks or used to build user profiles.

Access credentials will be handled locally and securely, subject to the final technical implementation and Pinterest's developer requirements.

5. Third-party services

Pinterest is an independent service subject to its own privacy policy and terms. Rulett does not control how Pinterest processes information within its platform.

6. Security

Appropriate technical measures will be implemented before the Pinterest integration is activated. No access tokens or private credentials will be published in the public source-code repository.

7. User rights

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning personal data processed by Rulett, contact: mariagavelasanzsanz@gmail.com.

You may also exercise the rights provided by applicable data protection legislation, including the GDPR.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application is implemented or its data processing practices change.
